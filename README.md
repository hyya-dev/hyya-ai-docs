## Two engines, one app

hYYa ai carries two engines, and you switch between them from one question to the next. You switch
engines, not apps.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔒 **hYYa Vault** — on your device | AI runs on your own Mac, iPhone, iPad or Android device. Your prompts, your replies and your history stay there. It answers with no connection and no account, and it is free forever. |
| ☁️ **hYYa Cloud** — online | Frontier models from several labs, live web research and reasoning across a whole document library, for when the work outgrows your device. |

Nothing you type, store or open in hYYa Vault reaches a server until you switch an online feature on — and
the first time you do, the app tells you what will leave and where it goes, then asks.

## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| 📄 **DataLens** | Ask your own PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, scans, and CSV, JSON and log files. Every answer points to the passage it came from, and scanned pages are read on your device too |
| 📊 **Studio** | Excel workbooks with working formulas, Word reports with a cover page and contents, PowerPoint with charts you can still edit, and interactive dashboards |
| 📐 **Quantity Surveyor** | Take quantities off construction drawings into Excel: DXF gives a full per-service take-off, vector PDF gives whole-drawing totals measured from the drawing's own geometry at the scale you set |
| 🧾 **Digitize** | Turn invoices, schedules and contracts into checked tables |
| ☀️ **Personal Assistant** | A morning Daily Brief of your meetings, reminders and the email that needs you, written on your device |
| 🌐 **Web Search & Connections** | Optional in hYYa Vault, off by default: only your question goes online, and the on-device model writes the answer from the results, with sources |
| 🧰 **Your own tools (MCP)** | Connect your own MCP tool servers and run them on your device — every call asks before it runs |
| 🎙️ **Voice & pictures** | Real-time voice, Live Translate and image generation — hYYa Cloud only. hYYa Vault has no microphone |

Turn any answer into a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or Markdown file, on either engine.

## Models

- **On your device (hYYa Vault):** open models from the Qwen, Gemma and Llama families, and Apple Intelligence
  on Apple devices where it is turned on. The app reads your device and recommends one model that fits; you tap
  once to download it, and nothing downloads on its own. The list of models is public and signed — see
  **[hyya-vault-manifest](https://github.com/hyya-dev/hyya-vault-manifest)**.
- **In the cloud (hYYa Cloud):** frontier models from several labs, among them Claude Opus, Claude Fable,
  Grok and Gemini.

## Pricing

Free to download. **hYYa Vault is free forever** and needs no account.

hYYa Cloud has two plans, which differ by how you pay, not by what you get: **Cloud On-Demand** (credit packs,
nothing renews, and the credits never expire) and **Cloud Ultra** (a monthly subscription). Current prices are
on [hyya.ai](https://hyya.ai).
